## Adversarial robustness

Cells are detection rates; the benign columns are false-positive rates on obfuscated harmless messages (content-guard: any category).

The **holdout** seeds were written after the normalizer was finished and were never used for tuning, so they are the fair comparison. The **dev** seeds are the ones the normalizer was developed against; treat that table as a regression suite.

## Keyboard mashing (synthetic)

There is no good public dataset for this, so positives are 400 generated keyboard mashes (seeded random walks over a QWERTY layout, see `generateMash` in this file) and negatives are 2,914 real messages: SMS "ham" (short, slangy, typo-heavy) and Stack Exchange titles. Treat it as a sanity check, not a benchmark.
